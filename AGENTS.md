## 3. The 6 Zero-Skip Retention Rules (MANDATORY for All Reels)
Every single script and animation sequence MUST strictly implement these 6 laws:

1. **Rule 1: The 1.2-Second Negative Contrast Hook**
   - Start immediately with an emotional contradiction or raw unsaid reality (e.g., *"A woman’s pain is loud until it turns into total silence. A man’s pain is silent from the very beginning."*).
   - Frame 0 must move immediately (progressive chalk stroke, spring pop, golden underline). Never open with greetings or slow intros.

2. **Rule 2: The Relatability Mirror**
   - Viscerally describe specific private actions (e.g., staring at a ceiling at 2:00 AM, typing three paragraphs and deleting them, smiling at work while dissociating).
   - Make the viewer feel seen: *"How do they know my exact life right now?"*

3. **Rule 3: Visual Novelty Every 2.5 Seconds**
   - No frame remains static for $>2.5$ seconds.
   - Framer Motion spring physics on all UI elements ($k=130-150, c=12-16$).
   - Constant micro-actions: Character breathing bob, eye blinks, drawstring sway, drifting ambient chalk motes.
   - Dynamic diagrams: Balance scales tipping, checklists checking off, battery meters draining.

4. **Rule 4: Tactical Audio Ducking & Sensory SFX**
   - Tactile ASMR chalk strokes on all text/diagram drawing.
   - Low-frequency heartbeat pulse at 58 BPM.
   - Deep baritone narration (`en-US-ChristopherNeural` at $-7\%$ rate, $-4Hz$ pitch).
   - The **"Zero-Volume Drop"**: Total acoustic silence for 0.8s right before the hardest hitting emotional truth.

5. **Rule 5: The Seamless Infinite Loop**
   - The final sentence of the script must grammatically and tonally connect back into the opening hook line.
   - Boosts completion rate beyond 100%, triggering Instagram explore page algorithms.

6. **Rule 6: The Story Repost & Share Trigger**
   - Close with an articulate, unsaid emotional truth (e.g., *"Send this to someone who needs to be heard"*).
   - People share content that speaks their hidden emotions for them.
